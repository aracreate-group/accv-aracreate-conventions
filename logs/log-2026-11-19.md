# Session flow, backlog and drive sources published

Work of 2026-11-19, committed 2026-11-19. The claude, repo and drive
conventions published from their source: a Claude Code session keeps its
work in the handoff's Todo until close, the repo's todo list becomes a flat
backlog with reminders, and the drive convention keeps dated snapshots of
external sources.

## Findings

- The leak scan of claude, repo and drive as committed found two session
  names used as examples in claude; they were made generic at the source,
  and the scan run again found none.

## Changed

- claude/: the handoff is an index of what a reload needs, under 100 lines,
  with session files beside it listed under Files; its Next becomes Todo,
  and ticked items stay until close. The todo list becomes a flat backlog
  with a reminders heading, done lists are retired, and close hands open
  work to the backlog. Sessions keep plain names and are dated at close,
  with links following a moved session's files. Terms gain `todo N` and
  `ask`, a fork is a separate session, and every reply that leaves a
  decision or next step ends on the question dialog. The todo template is
  replaced by a backlog template, and the handoff template gains Todo and
  Files.
- repo/: §5 names the local backlog.md in .session/.
- drive/: dated snapshots of external sources go in output/sources/.
